CYGNO04 v4 gas and sensitive geometry

All lengths are millimetres. The default geometry is now
../geometry/cygno04_v4_ASCII, with the reviewed full-cavity gas geometry.
To select original v3 with its legacy gas and sensitive boxes:
  CYGNO_GEOMETRY_PATH=../geometry/cygno_04_v3_ASCII ./CYGNO run.mac
Paths are relative to the process working directory. The variable overrides
only the startup CAD path; both updated CYGNO04 branch implementations support
v3 and v4 layouts. The eight required runtime STLs are N66, FieldCage, GEM, PEBase, PMMA,
Cu_radiopure, Cu_nonradiopure and GasBoundary. Generation scripts stay under
tools/.

CAD geometry is optional. If that directory is absent, CYGNO runs a nominal
analytic detector: a 660 x 920 x 1048 mm gas box and two 500 x 800 x 510 mm
sensitive boxes centred at Z=+255.030 and -255.030 mm. A 520 x 820 x 0.060 mm
cathode is placed at Z=0. Six 509 x 809 x 0.060 mm rectangular GEM foils have
centres Z=+/-(510.060, 512.120, 514.180) mm, leaving 510 mm between each
cathode face and the nearest GEM face. Cathode and GEMs use the same effective
GEM material as CAD mode. Gas mass excludes these material daughters; the
reported GEM mass includes all six foils.
This simple gas box is the nominal envelope proposed for the analytic mode;
it does not reproduce the complete PMMA cavity or its gas inventory. N66,
field cage, PMMA walls, PE base and CAD copper shields require the STL files.
Cameras, laboratory and analytic shielding keep their definitions.
The startup log identifies the selected mode.
If the CAD directory exists, all required meshes must be present and readable;
an incomplete directory produces an explicit error instead of falling back.
The following gas and active-volume descriptions apply to v4 CAD mode.

GasBoundary.stl follows the outer faces/contours of the main PMMA walls and
side covers. External fitting bores, feet and protruding optical frames are
outside that boundary. Geant4 subtracts the loaded PMMA.stl solid directly from
GasBoundary and assigns CYGNO_gas to the remaining volume. N66, FieldCage, GEM
and cathode daughters displace that gas. All enclosed empty cells inside this
boundary are included. This is not an inference that isolated CAD pockets share
the gas plumbing of the main chamber.

GasBoundary and PMMA are in the original PMMA CAD frame:
  detector X=CAD X; detector Y=CAD Z; detector Z=-CAD Y+1.39874923.
N66, FieldCage, GEM and PE keep the legacy frame:
  detector X=CAD X+470; detector Y=CAD Z; detector Z=-CAD Y.

Geant4 constructs each sensitive solid directly as a 500 x 800 x 510 mm
box minus the loaded N66 and field-cage solids, transformed into that box's
local coordinates. Their centres are Z=+255.030 and -255.030 mm, so they extend
from each cathode face to the first GEM face. No active-gas STL or extra
scoring clearance is required. Changing N66 or the cage updates these cuts
automatically. Both sensitive regions use CYGNO_gas; the remaining chamber
gas transports particles without sensitive scoring. Physical names remain
CYGNO_gas (copy 0 and 1); the mother is TPC_gas. The two active logical names
remain CYGNO_log and CYGNO_negative_log.

The current effective field cage and its transport material are unchanged.
N66.stl is the supplied minimal body with its 60-um seating planes restored,
matching field-cage clearance cuts and the lower-face rounding correction.
PMMA.stl and Cu_radiopure.stl use the accepted minimal exports. Source snapshots
and one-off N66 repair records are archived outside the runtime directory.
Gas and clearance changes do not assign isotope activities or change physics.

Approximate volumes:
  PMMA cavity before internal parts: about 779 L.
  Chamber gas after material displacement: about 746 L.
  Active gas per side: about 203.894 L (nominal bounding box: 204 L).
The gas total reported by Geant4 is a Boolean-solid volume estimate.
TPC_log mass alone excludes the active gas daughters; TPC_total_gas_mass and
TPC_total_gas_dens account for the complete gas inventory.

For future gas sources, /gps/pos/confine TPC_gas samples only non-active gas,
because GPS checks the deepest physical volume. To sample all gas uniformly,
combine TPC_gas and CYGNO_gas sources weighted by their gas volumes/masses, or
use a generator accepting all gas regions. Activities and normalization must
be specified separately.

Sensitive gas needs no mesh regeneration after changing N66 or the field cage.
To cut field-cage clearance into another N66 export, requires Python 3 with
numpy, trimesh and manifold3d (validated with 3.5.4). From the repository root:
  python3 tools/build_gas_geometry.py --geometry-dir cygno04_v4_ASCII --source-n66 /path/to/source_N66.stl --cut-n66 --skip-preview --output-dir /path/to/generated
Inspect and validate N66_clearance.stl before replacing N66.stl. This operation
does not restore seating planes lost by CAD mesh decimation. Omitting
--skip-preview also builds a visualization-only GasEnvelope_preview; it is not
used by Geant4.

GasBoundary is generated from the external PMMA(2).FCStd CAD source by
tools/build_gas_boundary.py. CAD/STEP sources are not needed to run Geant4.
To regenerate the boundary:
  CYGNO_PMMA_CAD=/path/to/source.FCStd FreeCADCmd tools/build_gas_boundary.py
 Output defaults to generated_gas_boundary under the
geometry repository. The script is specific to the supplied CAD object names
and main-wall dimensions; revise it if those CAD boundaries change.
Coordinate conventions are listed above. Source hashes are preserved with the
external validation records.
